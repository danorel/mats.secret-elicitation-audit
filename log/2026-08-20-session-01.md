## Known issues hit
- Sesterce/Shadeform boxes auto-launch a default `vllm/vllm-openai` container on
  boot that eats ~38/40GB VRAM — stop it first (`docker stop <name>`).
- `uv pip install` silently targets a root-owned `/opt/python-venv` unless you
  explicitly pass `--python .venv/bin/python` — a platform-wide `/etc/profile.d`
  script puts it first on PATH.
- `run_inference.sh`'s hardcoded `--batch_size 100` OOMs a 40GB card once the 9B
  model is loaded; 50 works.
- SAE feature extraction (`elicitation_methods/sae.py`) is ~10x slower per item
  than logit lens / Acts Tokens (~2.2s vs ~0.2s) — budget for it accordingly.
